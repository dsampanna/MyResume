About

I'm Sampanna Raj Dhungel — a Creative Director and Digital Media Designer based in Bhaktapur, Nepal. With 7+ years of hands-on experience, I work at the intersection of branding, UI/UX, motion graphics, 3D animation, and sound design.

From the first sketch to the final pixel, I care deeply about work that is intentional, clear, and built to last. I've had the privilege of working with 30+ clients locally and internationally — from startups finding their identity to established brands refreshing theirs.

When I'm not designing, I'm usually exploring new tools, writing about the craft, or listening to music that is way too loud.
