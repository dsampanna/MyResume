About HimalTrek

Our story

HimalTrek was founded in 2019 by a team of Kathmandu-based trekking professionals and technology experts who saw a gap: trekkers were struggling with fragmented information, complex permit processes, and unreliable safety tools.

We built HimalTrek to be the single platform that handles everything — from the moment you start dreaming about Nepal to the day you return home safely.

Today we've guided 12,400+ trekkers, processed 18,000+ permits, and partnered with 120 TAAN-certified guides across every region of Nepal.

Privacy policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use HimalTrek we collect: personal information you provide (name, email, passport number, nationality, emergency contact); booking and permit data; payment information processed securely via eSewa, Khalti or Stripe — we never store card numbers; device and usage data.

2. How we use your information

  • To process trek bookings and permit applications
  • To communicate with you about your trek (confirmations, guide assignment, weather alerts)
  • To submit permit applications to the Nepal Government (TAAN, Department of Immigration)
  • To improve our services and personalise your experience
  • To send optional marketing emails (opt out any time)

3. Data sharing

We share your data only with: TAAN-certified agencies managing your trek; Nepal Government bodies for permit processing; payment processors; emergency services if you activate SOS. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Passport and payment data is processed through PCI-DSS compliant systems. We perform annual security audits.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Email privacy@himaltrek.com — we respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand usage. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

7. Contact

privacy@himaltrek.com · HimalTrek Nepal Pvt. Ltd., Thamel, Kathmandu, Nepal

Terms of service

Last updated: April 20, 2026

1. Booking policy

  • Confirmed once payment received in full
  • Cancellation 72+ hrs before: full refund minus 5% processing fee
  • Cancellation 24–72 hrs before: 50% refund
  • Cancellation under 24 hrs: no refund
  • Force majeure (weather, political, natural disaster): full credit for future booking

2. Permits

Nepal Government permits are non-refundable once issued. HimalTrek processes applications as an agent and is not responsible for permit denials.

3. Safety & liability

Trekking in Nepal involves inherent risks. Clients must hold valid travel insurance including helicopter evacuation cover. HimalTrek and its guides are not liable for accidents, illness or natural disasters on trail.

4. Health requirements

Clients with pre-existing heart or respiratory conditions must obtain medical clearance. HimalTrek reserves the right to cancel a trek if a client is deemed medically unfit by the guide.

5. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Nepal. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Kathmandu.
