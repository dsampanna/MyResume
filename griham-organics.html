Our Story

From the Hills of Budanilkantha

Griham Organics was born from a simple belief — that the best food comes from the earth, made by hand, with love. Our pickles follow recipes passed down through generations, using ingredients grown in the clean mountain air of Budanilkantha.

  • ✓
    All ingredients sourced directly from certified organic farms in and around Budanilkantha, ensuring freshness and purity in every jar.
  • ✓
    We partner with local women farmers, providing fair wages and supporting sustainable livelihoods in rural Nepal.
  • ✓
    Zero artificial preservatives, colours or additives. Just real food made the way your grandmother would recognise.
  • ✓
    Packaged in eco-friendly glass jars that preserve flavour and reduce plastic waste in our communities.

How It's Made

From Farm to Your Table

Every jar of Griham Organics goes through a careful 4-step process to ensure the highest quality and most authentic flavour.

01
🌱
Organic Farming
Ingredients grown using traditional organic methods in Budanilkantha's fertile hillside farms.
02
🧺
Hand Harvesting
Carefully hand-picked at peak ripeness by our partner women farmers for maximum flavour.
03
🥄
Artisan Crafting
Prepared in small batches using traditional family recipes and hand-ground spices.
04
🫙
Glass Jar Sealing
Sealed in eco-friendly glass jars to preserve freshness with zero plastic waste.
